Model, assumptions and units

Battery energy = capacity × charge change. Grid energy = battery energy ÷ efficiency. Time = grid energy ÷ assumed grid input power. Cost = grid energy × price.

Assumes constant efficiency and linear charge percentage. Excludes tapering, thermal effects, conditioning, pauses, auxiliary loads and tariff changes. Reported charger power is not automatically wall power and is never prefilled. Price units are yours; no currency or tariff is inferred. Estimates are not guarantees.

About captures

A capture requests a vehicle reading and may incur API charges. No background history, no simulated timeline, no wake commands. Comparisons use only the two actual snapshots supplied by the local server.

Privacy and data

This interface only talks to your local dashboard server. No third-party requests, analytics, browser storage or location collection. Vehicles stay anonymous. The planner runs entirely in your browser.

Opening this page loads cached data only. Refresh and capture are explicit manual reads; API charges may apply. Nothing auto-polls, wakes a vehicle or sends vehicle commands. Estimates and readings are provided without warranties.
